When to run it. Before both sandbag and kettlebell sessions — it preps the exact joints either will tax. On a true off day, do it on its own as a slower, exploratory movement snack rather than a warm-up.

The one rule. If it ever feels like a workout you need to recover from, you've made it too hard. Easy, curious, frequent.
