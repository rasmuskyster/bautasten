Sandbag protocol

One lift, then its carry, then the bells. 90 s rest between lift sets, 60 s between carries — tap a set and the timer starts itself. The bag works in three positions — bearhug, lap, shoulder. Session A runs deadlift + row through day 18, then graduates to the shouldered squat.

The valve. Poor sleep or heavy legs? Cut the carry to one set or skip it. The lift is the priority; the carry comes off first.

Variations (same sets, swap when you want variety): bearhug reverse lunge as a squat-day alternative (4 × 3 per side) · sandbag bent-over row as a swap for the early-phase deadlift or an extra pull (3 × 6–8). Skip slams — the Dual-Ply isn't built for them — and high-rep hang cleans, which don't work on a handleless 60 kg bag.

Reading the signals

The trimmed kettlebell reps should feel fast and powerful. If swings turn sluggish across sessions, trim sandbag volume before kettlebell volume. Arriving sore? Pull the carry first. Heavy ground-to-shoulder days (C) deliberately avoid swings and lawn mowers — keep that pairing if you reorder.
