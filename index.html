Kettlebell weight key

Suggested starting points. Set your own kg on any movement in the Today view — it's remembered per movement and intensity across all 30 days.

Sandbag protocol

One lift, then its carry, then the bells. 90 s rest between lift sets, 60 s between carries — tap a set and the timer starts itself. The bag works in three positions — bearhug, lap, shoulder — and the 60 kg never changes.

The valve. Poor sleep or heavy legs? Cut the carry to one set or skip it. The lift is the priority; the carry comes off first.

Progression
Reading the signals

The trimmed kettlebell reps should feel fast and powerful. If swings turn sluggish across sessions, trim sandbag volume before kettlebell volume. Arriving sore? Pull the carry first.
