Current cycle

Archiving snapshots this cycle's totals and weights, then resets the 30 days. Weight settings carry into the next cycle.

The method

Richard Love's Savage Army, kettlebell only. Each day is a circuit of ballistic movements at your ~200-rep pace. Do your own warm-up first, work through the movements resting as needed, and finish with a few minutes of breathing.

Kettlebell weight key

Suggested starting points. Set your own kg on any movement in Today — remembered per movement and intensity across all 30 days.

Movement notes

Tactical cleans. Diagonal clean that switches hands mid-lift and finishes with an overhead press — overhead finish, so lighter.

Figure 8s. Both hands on the bell, swung diagonally across the front of the body while shifting weight side to side. Taxing — treat it as real conditioning.

Lawn mower starters. Diagonal pull across the body, finishing standing side-on — whole-body rotation, not an isolated row.

Chop ups. Bell held low at one side, driven diagonally across and up to straight arms overhead. An explosive low-to-high diagonal.

Weight selection

Pick the weight where each broken set stays powerful with 2–3 reps in reserve — never grind a swing or snatch to failure. Overhead finishes (snatch, thruster, presses, tactical cleans) cap lower; braced pulls (rows) take the most.
